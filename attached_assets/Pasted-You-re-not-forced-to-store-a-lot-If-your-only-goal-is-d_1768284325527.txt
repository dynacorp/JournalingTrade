You’re not forced to store a lot. If your only goal is **“drawdown per trade”**, you can keep the DB lean and still get what you want.

## What you actually need to store (minimal)

For each *closed trade event*, store **only**:

### Identity + time (for grouping + dedupe)

* `deal_id` (unique from MT5)
* `account_id`
* `symbol`
* `side` (BUY/SELL)
* `open_time_utc`
* `close_time_utc`

### Entry/exit (needed to interpret MAE)

* `entry_price`
* `close_price`
* `volume` *(optional, but useful if you ever want money-based MAE)*

### The drawdown you care about (MAE)

* `mae_points` **(this is your “per-trade drawdown”)**
* `mae_price` *(optional but nice for debugging/verification)*
* `mae_time_utc` *(optional but nice)*

That’s it.

If you store just `mae_points`, you can build your UI and analytics around “worst heat per trade” with almost no storage.

---

## Strong recommendation: keep ONE extra field

Even if you only care about drawdown, store **risk at entry** so you can normalize it:

* `risk_points` (distance from entry to SL, if SL exists)

Then you can show:

* `mae_R = mae_points / risk_points`

This is insanely helpful because “120 points drawdown” means nothing unless you know your planned stop distance.

If you truly don’t want it, you can drop it—but it’s small and high value.

---

## What you should NOT store (if you want a clean DB)

Unless you need them later, you can skip:

* Tick history (huge)
* MFE (favorable excursion)
* All OHLC snapshots
* Comments/magic
* Every SL/TP modification event
* Screenshots

You can still compute MAE in MT5 and only ship the final MAE values.

---

## Minimal payload to send to your server

Send only this JSON on trade close:

* `deal_id`, `account_id`
* `symbol`, `side`
* `open_time_utc`, `close_time_utc`
* `entry_price`, `close_price`
* `mae_points`
* *(optional)* `mae_price`, `mae_time_utc`
* *(optional)* `risk_points`

---

## Practical tip: store “points” not “money”

For synthetic indices, “points” is stable for analysis and avoids broker/currency confusion.

If you later want money metrics, you can compute using `volume` + contract size—but you don’t need it for per-trade drawdown.

---

### Bottom line

If the only feature is “drawdown per trade,” your DB can be very small:

* **1 row per trade**
* **MAE points** (+ optional risk_points)
